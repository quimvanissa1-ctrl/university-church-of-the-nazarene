Who We Are

Discover our history, our faith, and the people who serve our church family.

Our History

University Church of the Nazarene has a long heritage of gospel ministry, local service, and global compassion. From our earliest days, our congregation has gathered to worship, learn, and reach out with practical love.

Our Faith & Practices

Rooted in Scripture and Wesleyan-Holiness tradition, we believe in grace, holiness, and the transforming power of Jesus Christ. Our worship, prayer, and discipleship practices are designed to grow Christ-like character in every member.

Our Staff

Our pastors, ministry leaders, and support team are committed to shepherding the church, equipping volunteers, and caring for families. Together, they bring pastoral care, teaching, and direction for every season of ministry.
